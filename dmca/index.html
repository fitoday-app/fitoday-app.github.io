Fitoday Copyright & DMCA Policy

Effective date: Draft, not yet in effect

Fitoday respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users to do the same. We respond to notices of alleged copyright infringement following the notice-and-takedown process of the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 512), and to notices under Canada's Copyright Act (notice-and-notice regime).

Where to send a notice

Send copyright notices by email to fitonedayreport22@gmail.com.

This email address is only for copyright notices. Other messages will not get a response; please use fitoneday22@gmail.com instead.

How to file a takedown notice

Your notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full legal name is acceptable).
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  3. Identification of the infringing material and enough information for us to find it, such as the Fitoday username and a description of the entry or a screenshot.
  4. Your contact information: name, address, phone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

Knowingly making false claims may make you liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees.

What we do when we receive a valid notice

  • We remove or disable access to the material, usually within 24–48 hours.
  • We notify the user who posted it and give them a copy of the notice (your contact information may be included).
  • We record the notice against the user's account for our repeat-infringer policy.

Counter-notice

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the same email address containing:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the removed material and where it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the U.S. federal district court for the judicial district where your address is located (or, if your address is outside the U.S., any judicial district in which Fitoday may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notice or their agent.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we will forward it to the original complainant. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we may restore the content 10 to 14 business days after receiving the counter-notice.

Canadian notice-and-notice

Copyright owners may also send a notice under the Copyright Act (Canada). If we receive a notice that meets the legal requirements, we will forward it to the user involved as required by law.

Repeat infringers

We terminate the accounts of users who are subject to repeated valid infringement notices. As a guideline, an account with three valid notices that were not successfully countered will be permanently banned. We may terminate sooner for clear or serious infringement.

Trademarks and other rights

For trademark, privacy or impersonation complaints, contact fitoneday22@gmail.com or use the in-app Report button.
