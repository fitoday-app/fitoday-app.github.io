Fitoday Privacy Policy

Effective date: Draft, not yet in effect
Version: 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains what information yedam lee ("Fitoday", "we", "us") collects through the Fitoday app (the "App"), why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

Short version:

  • We collect only what the App needs to work: your account, your outfit diary, your ratings and your settings.
  • Entries set to "Only me" and your private notes are only ever visible to you.
  • We do not sell your data, use ad tracking, track your location, read your contacts, or use your photos to train AI. We show a few ads in Discover and search, never in your diary, and they are not personalized.
  • We do not store your date of birth. We only use it once to confirm you are 16 or older.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the App.

2. Camera and photo library

The App asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you log an outfit or set a profile picture. We only upload the single photo you select. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App: accounts, your outfit diary, streaks, ratings, scores, points, levels, search, follows and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, automatically screening uploaded images and text for prohibited content, preventing spam and fake accounts, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To secure the service, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To communicate with you about your account, safety issues or changes to our Terms or this Policy. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your account or diary information for advertising, profiling for advertising, or selling. We do not use your photos or content to train AI models.

Automated screening

Uploaded images and text are automatically checked for prohibited content (such as nudity or offensive language) using an automated filter. The filter is run by us or by the service provider listed in Section 5, which may only use your photo to perform the check and may not keep it or use it for its own purposes (including AI training). Flagged content may be hidden until a human reviews it. This is the only automated decision we make about your content; if you think your content was flagged by mistake, contact us and a person will review it.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in Canada (Central). Service providers may process data in Canada, the United States or other countries. When that happens, your data may be accessible to authorities in those countries under their laws.

To work without a connection, the App keeps a copy of your own diary on your phone (entries, private notes, thumbnails, streak and profile). It stays out of iCloud backups and is removed when you sign out or delete your account. Other people's content is not saved this way.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: while your account is active.
  • After you delete your account: your profile, diary entries, photos, private notes, item tags, follows and saved items are deleted immediately from the live service and removed from backups within 30 days. Ratings you gave others remain only as anonymous numbers with no link to you, so other users' scores stay accurate.
  • Reports and moderation records: up to 1 year, or longer if needed for legal reasons.
  • Banned accounts: we keep a one-way hashed (scrambled) version of the email or Apple identifier to prevent the banned person from signing up again.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Copyright notices: as long as required to comply with copyright law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile in the App. Email us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete account. You can also email us.
  • Privacy: switch your account to private at any time.
  • Notifications: turn off any notification type in Settings or in your device settings.
  • Permissions: change camera and photo access in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent by deleting your account; some features cannot work without the data described above.

Depending on where you live (for example, Canada or certain U.S. states), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your data, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We will respond to requests within 30 days. To make a request, email fitoneday22@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first.

If you are in Canada and are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children

Fitoday is not for anyone under 16. We block sign-up for users who are under 16. If someone enters a birthday under 16, we do not store the birthday. We keep only their email address and a blocked status, so the same email cannot be used to sign up. They can ask us to remove it at fitoneday22@gmail.com. If we learn that an existing user is under 16, we will delete their account. If you believe a child under 16 is using the App, contact fitoneday22@gmail.com.

10. Security

We protect your data using encryption in transit (HTTPS), access rules that limit each user to the data they are allowed to see, server-side checks on all important actions, limited administrator access, and regular security testing. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal information and creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11. Advertising and tracking

The App shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob. Ads only appear outside your diary: one ad after every few posts in the Discover feed, and one under search results. They never appear in your calendar, entries, stats or logging screens.

All ads are non-personalized. We do not ask for permission to track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), we do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), and we never share your account, diary, photos or notes with advertisers. To show and measure ads, Google receives technical data from your device such as your IP address, device model and OS version, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this as described in its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Because some users are 16 or 17, we only allow ads suitable for teens.

The App contains no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or session-replay tools.

12. Changes to this Policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. For significant changes, we will notify you in the App.
