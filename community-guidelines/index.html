Fitoday Community Guidelines

Effective date: Draft, not yet in effect

Fitoday is your daily outfit diary, and a place to share outfits and get honest, kind feedback if you want to. These Guidelines are part of our Terms of Service. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

Log outfits, and only outfits

  • Every entry should show an outfit you are wearing or styled.
  • Post your own photos. Don't repost other people's photos, brand campaign images, screenshots or memes.
  • Don't post photos of other people without their permission.
  • One account per person. Don't create extra accounts to log more or rate yourself.
  • These rules apply to every entry, including entries set to "Only me" and private notes.

Never allowed

Uploading any of these leads to removal and, in most cases, a permanent ban:

  • Nudity or sexual content, including see-through, lingerie or swimwear photos posed in a sexual way.
  • Any sexualized content involving minors. We report this to authorities.
  • Violence, gore, weapons used to threaten, or self-harm content.
  • Hate speech or symbols attacking people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age or any other protected trait.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or body shaming.
  • Illegal activity or illegal goods, including drugs.
  • Sharing someone's personal information (addresses, phone numbers, school, etc.).
  • Spam, scams, ads, links to sell products, or promotion of other accounts or services.
  • Impersonating people or brands.
  • Content that infringes copyrights or trademarks.

Rating and feedback

  • Rate the outfit, not the person. Comments about someone's body, face, weight, skin, race or gender are not allowed.
  • Use Improve when you have a real suggestion. Feedback notes should help: "A shorter jacket would balance the wide pants" ✅ — "ugly" ❌.
  • Don't coordinate with others to rate someone up or down, and don't trade ratings for points.
  • Don't farm points. Don't spam feedback notes (copy-pasted, filler or meaningless text) or rate entries without really looking just to earn points. Notes removed for spam lose their points.
  • Feedback notes are private to the entry owner but are still covered by these Guidelines.

Usernames and profiles

No offensive, sexual or hateful usernames, profile pictures or captions, and no impersonation.

Keeping Fitoday safe

  • Report anything that breaks these rules: tap ••• on an entry or profile → Report.
  • Block anyone you don't want to interact with. They won't be able to see or rate your entries, and you won't see theirs.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. Entries that receive several reports may be hidden automatically while we review them.
  • Uploaded photos and text are automatically screened, and flagged content may be held for review.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: content removal, a warning, points reset, temporary suspension, or a permanent ban. Severe violations lead to an immediate permanent ban. If you think we made a mistake, email fitoneday22@gmail.com.

If you're in danger

Fitoday cannot respond to emergencies. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in Canada and the U.S.).
